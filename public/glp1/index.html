Clinical safety

Side effects, managed from day one

GLP-1 works best when it's supervised. Without a plan, people stay on the wrong dose, eat too little protein and give up muscle along with fat. Every part of Sthira is built to prevent that.

  • Nausea and GI side effectsDoses go up slowly and are held or stepped back if side effects flare. Never a fixed schedule on autopilot.
  • Muscle protectionWithout supervision, a large share of the weight that comes off can be muscle. Protein targets and strength training are part of every plan.
  • Rebound protectionLonger plans end with a doctor-guided taper and habits built to last beyond the medication.

Questions

Before you join

Is this a GLP-1 plan?

Yes. It's a doctor-led GLP-1 plan: the medication, titrated by your doctor, with side-effect management and a protein and strength protocol to protect your muscle. GLP-1 is prescribed only after your blood test and consult. If you aren't eligible, you get a full refund except the blood test.

What doses will I receive?

Your doctor starts you on a low dose and reviews it every month. It goes up only as your body tolerates it, and is held or stepped back if side effects flare. Custom titration is included in your plan price.

Can I get GLP-1 without a prescription?

No. GLP-1 medicines are prescription-only in India. You need an assessment by a registered doctor, with blood work, before you start.

Who shouldn't take GLP-1?

It isn't suitable for everyone. Doctors usually avoid it during pregnancy and with certain thyroid or pancreas conditions. Your doctor checks your history and reports before prescribing.

What side effects should I expect?

Nausea and digestive discomfort are common in the first few weeks and usually settle. Doses go up slowly, and your doctor holds or steps back the dose if side effects flare.

Will GLP-1 cost me muscle?

Muscle loss is a known risk when GLP-1 is used without supervision. That's why every plan includes a protein target set with your doctor and a strength routine, and why we track muscle, not just weight.

Do I need GLP-1 forever?

Many people use GLP-1 for a defined period. Longer plans end with a doctor-guided taper, while the habits and muscle you've built help your results hold.

Who are the doctors?

The clinical protocol is designed and supervised by a physician with an MD in Internal Medicine. Every prescription is written by a qualified specialist after a consult and blood work.

What results can I expect?

We won't promise a number. Results depend on your starting point, your health and how closely you follow the plan. We measure carefully and publish our members' real results.

Where is it available?

Doctor consults and check-ins happen on video and WhatsApp, so you can join from anywhere in India. We'll confirm home blood tests and medicine delivery for your pin code before you start.

What happens to my information?

Your answers are used only to assess fit and contact you about the programme. We don't sell or share them. To have them deleted, email hello@tinkrlabs.ai or reply "delete" to any of our WhatsApp messages.
