Questions

Before you join

Is this a medication programme?

It's a doctor-led programme where coaching, food and strength come first. Treatment is included only if your doctor prescribes it after your blood test and consult. If it isn't right for you, you get a refund except the blood test, or you can continue on coaching and nutrition alone.

Who are the doctors?

The clinical protocol is designed and supervised by a physician with an MD in Internal Medicine. Every prescription is written by a qualified specialist after a consult and blood work.

What results can I expect?

We won't promise a number. Results depend on your starting point, your health and how closely you follow the plan. We measure carefully and publish every cohort's real results.

What happens after 6 months?

You can move to Maintain: monthly weigh-ins and quarterly blood tests. If you were on treatment, your doctor steps it down gradually while your habits take over.

Where is it available?

The first cohort is in Delhi NCR, with blood tests at home. Consults and coaching are online, so more cities will follow.

What happens to my information?

Your answers are used only to assess fit and contact you about the programme. We don't sell or share them. To have them deleted, email hello@tinkrlabs.ai or reply "delete" to any of our WhatsApp messages.
